A Move-Cleaning Checklist for Cullman, AL Homes: Kitchens, Baths and Handover

A useful move-cleaning checklist for a Cullman, AL home breaks the property into kitchen, bathroom and living-space tasks, then adds the timing of packing and handover. Titan Cleaning Company’s move-cleaning service covers appliance and storage interiors, bathroom fixtures and tile, floors and baseboards. Keep this as a written planning resource, not a guarantee about a deposit or an automatically accepted service specification.

Kitchen checklist: appliances and cabinet interiors

Create separate kitchen entries for counters, stovetop, oven interior, refrigerator and freezer interiors, and cabinet interiors. Titan’s move-cleaning service includes degreasing the range hood, cleaning behind appliances and under the sink, and cleaning inside cabinets, closets and drawers. Put closet and drawer interiors under the relevant rooms rather than hiding them in a broad ‘kitchen done’ or ‘whole house’ note. Confirm any cabinet-exterior or windowsill request separately so the agreed scope covers the surfaces you want cleaned.

Identify which areas will be clear of stored items when the cleaning takes place. Cabinet interiors and a countertop are different work areas; access to one says nothing about the other. Record the requested work clearly before treating the checklist as complete.

Bathroom checklist: fixtures, tile and mirrors

For each bathroom, list the toilet, sink, bathtub or shower, tile and mirror. Titan cleans those areas, along with soap scum and hard-water buildup, exhaust fans and light covers. If grout needs particular attention, describe its condition and ask about that task rather than treating grout work from another cleaning description as an automatic move-service inclusion. A room-by-room record helps distinguish ordinary recent use from persistent residue.

Keep maintenance defects separate from cleaning tasks. Damaged grout, failed sealant or a scratched surface may remain after soil is removed. A cleaning checklist can record those observations, but it is not a promise to repair materials or restore every surface to new condition.

Living-space checklist: floors, edges and touchpoints

Titan’s move-cleaning service includes sweeping, mopping or vacuuming floors; wiping baseboards; and cleaning light switches, outlet covers and door frames. It also mentions the washer-and-dryer area, garage entry door and utility closets. Use those as distinct checklist entries instead of treating floor cleaning as the whole room. Describe any furniture dusting, wall washing or windowsill work as a separate request to confirm before booking.

Keep window washing separate from floor and room-edge cleaning. Window washing can be added for an extra charge. Identify the windows and the work requested, and ask for that addition to be included in the scope and quote. A general note about cleaning near a window does not establish agreement to wash the glass.

Sequence the clean around packing and furniture

Titan explains that many Cullman, AL customers arrange move-out cleaning after furniture has been removed so floors, walls and closets are accessible; for move-in work, its stated aim is to clean before the moving truck arrives. Use that access rationale when planning packing and handover, but confirm an available appointment rather than assuming the day after a move is guaranteed. Repeated moving traffic can otherwise put fresh mess on cleaned surfaces.

Record expected room access and any remaining belongings. A mostly empty room can expose edges that were previously blocked, but do not assume cleaners will move heavy furniture or dispose of possessions. Confirm the cleaning appointment and arrange furniture removal separately.

Walk through the property before handover

Before handing over keys, compare the requested checklist with the visible condition of the property. Look at the less obvious entries as well as the floors: cabinet interiors, mirrors, appliance areas and room edges are easy to overlook in a quick walk-through.

Document outstanding cleaning issues and distinguish them from damage or maintenance matters. A move-out clean cannot establish what a landlord will deduct or guarantee a deposit refund. Lease or sale obligations remain separate from this practical cleaning resource.

Agree the scope, estimate and handover window before booking

Titan identifies home size, property condition and appliance work as factors affecting cleaning time and cost. It lists garage cleaning, patio or deck cleaning and window washing as extra-charge additions. Describe any heavy kitchen buildup, pet-related issues and additional areas before comparing a quote. Do not assume that a broad room label includes every optional task.

Use the same room-and-task list when comparing estimates. Ask which work is included, which requests are additions and how any change will be agreed before extra work begins. Confirm when furniture removal will finish, the available cleaning appointment and the deadline for checking the property before key handover. A lower initial figure is not a like-for-like comparison if it excludes requested appliance work or window washing. Confirm the quote and expected timing directly with Titan; deposit decisions remain with the landlord.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Titan’s move cleaning include cabinet interiors?

Yes. Titan cleans inside all cabinets, closets and drawers, including shelves, rods and interior storage walls. It also describes oven, refrigerator and freezer interiors. Confirm the exact cabinet surfaces and tasks for your property before booking.

Is window cleaning the same as wiping windowsills?

No. Window washing is a separate request, and Titan offers it as an extra-charge addition. Confirm the windows, work and quote. Ask separately about windowsills so the quote reflects the work you want.

Can move-out cleaning guarantee a deposit return?

Cleaning does not guarantee the return of a security deposit. A landlord's decision may involve lease terms, damage and other matters beyond cleaning.
