A Move-Cleaning Checklist for Cullman, AL Homes: Kitchens, Baths and Handover

A useful move-cleaning checklist for a Cullman, AL home breaks the property into kitchen, bathroom and living-space tasks, then adds the timing of packing and handover. Titan Cleaning Company's move-in/move-out page provides a concrete starting list, including appliance work, cabinet interiors, bathroom tile and grout, floors and baseboards. Keep this as a written planning resource, not a guarantee about a deposit or an automatically accepted service specification.

Kitchen checklist: appliances and cabinet interiors

Create separate kitchen entries for counters, stovetop, oven interior, refrigerator and freezer interiors, cabinet interiors, cabinet exteriors and windowsills. Titan's move-cleaning description also identifies storage work inside cabinets, closets and drawers. Put closet and drawer interiors under the relevant rooms rather than hiding them in a broad 'kitchen done' or 'whole house' note.

As general preparation, identify which areas will be clear of stored items when the cleaning takes place. Cabinet interiors and a countertop are different work areas; access to one says nothing about the other. Record the requested work clearly before treating the checklist as complete.

Bathroom checklist: fixtures, tile and mirrors

For each bathroom, list the toilet, sink, bathtub or shower, tile, grout and mirror. Titan names those areas in its move-cleaning page. A room-by-room record is useful when one bathroom has ordinary recent use while another has persistent residue.

Keep maintenance defects separate from cleaning tasks. Damaged grout, failed sealant or a scratched surface may remain after soil is removed. A cleaning checklist can record those observations, but it is not a promise to repair materials or restore every surface to new condition.

Living-space checklist: floors, edges and touchpoints

Titan's living-space list includes vacuuming and mopping floors; dusting furniture, windowsills and baseboards; and wiping walls, light switches and outlets. Use those as distinct checklist entries instead of treating floor cleaning as the whole room.

Keep window glass separate from windowsills. Titan's move-service FAQ says window cleaning is available if requested. A sill entry alone should not imply that glass cleaning has been requested or agreed. This distinction makes a written checklist more useful than a general instruction to clean every window area.

Sequence the clean around packing and furniture

Titan explains that many Cullman, AL customers arrange move-out cleaning after furniture has been removed so floors, walls and closets are accessible; for move-in work, its stated aim is to clean before the moving truck arrives. Use that access rationale when planning packing and handover, but confirm an available appointment rather than assuming the day after a move is guaranteed. Repeated moving traffic can otherwise put fresh mess on cleaned surfaces.

Record expected room access and any remaining belongings. A mostly empty room can expose edges that were previously blocked, but do not assume cleaners will move heavy furniture or dispose of possessions. This sequencing advice is not a Titan availability commitment or a moving-service inclusion.

Walk through the property before handover

Before handing over keys, compare the requested checklist with the visible condition of the property. Look at the less obvious entries as well as the floors: cabinet interiors, mirrors, appliance areas and room edges are easy to overlook in a quick walk-through.

Document outstanding cleaning issues and distinguish them from damage or maintenance matters. A move-out clean cannot establish what a landlord will deduct or guarantee a deposit refund. Lease or sale obligations remain separate from this practical cleaning resource.
